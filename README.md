## Five nights at Freddy game series

** Five nights at Freddy - a game made by Scott Cawthon, an American independent video game developer, animator, and writer. He developed and published the first survival horror game on August 8, 2014, which quickly became a global phenomenon and spawned a massive media franchise including sequels, books, merchandise, and a film adaptation released in 2023.

Setting: Utah, USA

Time Period: July 4 2029 (Independence Day)

Main Character: Gregory, Glamrock Freddy
Gregory is the main character you play as. He’s a young boy who gets trapped inside the Mega Pizzaplex after closing time. With no family or friends around, he’s forced to survive until morning while being hunted by hostile animatronics and the mysterious figure Vanny. Motivation: His main motivation is to survive and escape danger, protect those he cares about, and stop the evil forces from hurting anyone else.
